**Everyone talks about unicorns. The rainbow never gets a good part in the story. That changes tonight.**
<img src="https://github.com/LazyLuigi/roygbiv-js13k/raw/main/media/gameplay.gif"/>

Roygbiv  is a **80's like top-down roguelite shooter** where you play the rainbow, armed with a pump-action shotgun, against a horde of sickeningly cute unicorns.

## The Core Mechanic:

The hero's body is made of seven colored bands, and those seven bands are both your health bar and your seven power slots.

- You start completely gray, with only one randomly selected band painted.
- Every time you level up, one band is painted and gains a power, chosen from three randomly drawn abilities out of the four available for that color.
- Taking a hit extinguishes the innermost band: you lose its power along with it, and your best card is always the first one to go.
- Go four seconds without taking damage and the band is restored, together with its power.

There are 28 powers, four for each color band, and seven tiers of unicorns, including a mother unicorn that splits into two when killed.

At the end, you face a four-headed hydra whose mane is made of seven stolen rainbow locks. Each lock you tear away kills one head: its eyes turn into Xs and its neck collapses to the ground !

## Controls

| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| Arrows or WASD / ZQSD | Move |
| Touch anywhere | Virtual joystick |
| 1, 2, 3 | Choose a power |
| M | Toggle sound |

Aiming and firing are automatic. The shotgun fires only when an enemy is within range.
